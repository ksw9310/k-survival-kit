RENT SYSTEM
WOLSE VS
JEONSE
→
Wolse — monthly rent + small deposit
Most common for students
→
Jeonse — huge lump-sum deposit, no monthly rent
Millions of KRW upfront — avoid for now
⚠️ Never pay deposit without a written contract
